About

About me

Most of what I build is the same problem in different shapes: not enough compute, sensors that disagree, and a messy environment — yet the perception → discrimination → decision loop still has to close in milliseconds. The answer isn't a bigger model. It's splitting the task into independently schedulable perception skills with heterogeneous deployment targets, then orchestrating them against resource and accuracy constraints — so you pay for less compute, and a DSP can run on the ARM core while convolutions run on the NPU.

At SAST, the multi-source fusion refactor I lead has passed review by SASTIND, the national defence-industry regulator, saving over ¥1M on a single project with an official application certificate. I also followed the full delivery chain end to end: proposal → design review → model training → migrating algorithms onto hardware → post-launch quality maintenance. Earlier, at Huantian Smart Technology and the Turing AI Research Institute, I completed the perception-to-planning loop. I hold 2 granted and 6 pending patents, one SCI Q2 paper and two EI-indexed papers.

Outside work I turn hard-won lessons into reusable, open-source tooling: a research-proposal skill pack for defence and national programmes with built-in mechanical compliance checks, and a job-driven knowledge pipeline that scrapes postings, extracts concepts, and ships daily increments.

FishBot Mecanum Robot: LiDAR–Vision Fused Following

On the FishBot v2 mecanum-wheel robot, I implemented spatio-temporal alignment and fusion of LiDAR and camera data in ROS 2 — target detection, motion-state estimation and behavior prediction feeding a follow decision and motion command. The hardware stack spans STM32 / ESP32 microcontrollers and an Ascend 310B (Atlas DK2) edge module. The core problem is target loss under occlusion and abrupt lighting changes, where any single sensor alone falls short.
