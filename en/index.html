Embodied AI · Personal 2026

FishBot Mecanum Robot: LiDAR–Vision Fused Following

On the FishBot v2 mecanum-wheel robot, I implemented spatio-temporal alignment and fusion of LiDAR and camera data in ROS 2 — target detection, motion-state estimation and behavior prediction feeding a follow decision and motion command. The hardware stack spans STM32 / ESP32 microcontrollers and an Ascend 310B (Atlas DK2) edge module. The core problem is target loss under occlusion and abrupt lighting changes, where any single sensor alone falls short.
