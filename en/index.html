About

About me

My research focuses on multimodal data fusion and embodied AI. The work centres on perception under constrained embedded compute: heterogeneous sensors sampled asynchronously, in environments with occlusion, illumination change and electromagnetic interference. The approach is to decompose the perception pipeline into independently schedulable modules with heterogeneous deployment targets, then select an execution path under resource and accuracy constraints.

At SAST I am responsible for a multi-source fusion algorithm refactor that brings point-cloud, infrared and visible-light data into a unified processing framework, replacing the previous cascaded pipeline. The approach passed review by SASTIND and saved the client over ¥1 million on a single project, with an official application certificate. I took part in the full project lifecycle: proposal argumentation, design review, model training, migration of algorithms onto a domestic hardware platform, and post-launch quality maintenance. Earlier, at Huantian Smart Technology and the Turing AI Research Institute, I developed algorithms for perception, prediction and planning.

I hold 2 granted and 6 pending patents, with one SCI Q2 paper, two EI-indexed papers and one SPIE conference paper. Outside work I maintain open-source tooling for reusable engineering methods, including a research-proposal writing and checking toolkit and a job-posting analysis and knowledge-organisation toolchain.

boss_find_skill: job-posting analysis toolchain for tracking technological development

A toolchain for tracking technological development through job postings. The analysis stage computes statistics over posting text and extracts high-frequency terms, identifying newly emerging technical directions and engineering concepts from changes in hiring requirements. The learning stage organises those concepts into spaced-repetition cards for continuous follow-up. The toolchain also provides resume-based match scoring and a ranked shortlist.

proposal-generator-cn: research-proposal writing skill package

A skill package that assists in writing research proposals, applicable to national research programmes and general research project applications. The body text follows an academic register without meta-commentary, technical metrics carry their own baseline arguments, and unresolved items are separated into an attached checklist. Three executable tools are included for style compliance checking, Markdown to Word conversion, and Word structure verification.
